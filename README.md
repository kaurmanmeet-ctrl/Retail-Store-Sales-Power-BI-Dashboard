# Retail-Store-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard delivering end-to-end sales performance analytics across products, cities, and time — built to support data-driven retail decision-making.

📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard that consolidates multi-year transactional data into a single, unified view. It enables business stakeholders to monitor revenue performance, track top-selling products, identify high-value geographies, and analyze sales trends over time — all through a clean, interactive interface.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Microsoft Power BI was used as the primary platform for dashboard design, data modeling, and building interactive visualizations.
All custom KPI calculations, Year-on-Year comparisons, and business measures were written in DAX (Data Analysis Expressions). 
Raw data was cleaned, transformed, and structured into a reliable reporting layer using Power Query (M Language) as the ETL pipeline. 
Source data was maintained in Microsoft Excel / CSV format for pre-processing and storage. 
The underlying data architecture follows a Star Schema, with clearly defined relationships between fact and dimension tables to ensure accurate aggregations across all visuals.

📊 Key KPIs
The dashboard tracks five core performance indicators across all categories and time periods.
Total Revenue stands at ₹12.95 Crore, reflecting cumulative sales across all product categories.
A total of 7,125 units were sold across all products, with an Average Order Value of ₹36,893 per transaction — indicating a high-value, premium product mix.
The business served 50 unique active buyers over the tracked period. 
2023 emerged as the best performing year, recording ₹3.53 Crore in revenue — the highest across all four years analyzed.

💡 Key Insights


2023 was the strongest year, generating ₹3.53 Cr in revenue — a 19% recovery from the 2022 dip of ₹2.97 Cr.
Electronics is the dominant category, contributing approximately 74% of total revenue, indicating a clear market preference in this retail segment.
Apple products lead product-level performance, with iPhone 14 and MacBook Air together contributing over ₹4.33 Cr — roughly 33% of total revenue.
Tier-2 cities outperform metros — Bhopal and Kanpur rank above Mumbai, suggesting strong untapped demand in emerging urban markets.
Revenue dips sharply in early 2024 (January), pointing to possible post-holiday seasonality or inventory/supply issues worth monitoring.
Average Order Value of ₹36,893 reflects a high-value, low-volume sales model, consistent with a premium electronics-focused product mix.
